Under the Hood

A Simple, Explainable Pipeline

AssignCheck breaks every submission into small, comparable pieces of text and measures how much they overlap. No black boxes — every score traces back to exact phrases.

N-Grams

Text is split into overlapping word sequences — for example energy from the sun and from the sun drives. When two documents share many n-grams, they reuse the same phrases.

Jaccard Similarity

The shared n-grams between two documents, divided by their total unique n-grams. The result becomes a similarity score between 0% and 100%.

The Water Cycle

Essay · Page 1 of 4 · Excerpt

The water cycle describes the continuous movement of water on, above and below the surface of the Earth. Energy from the sun drives evaporation, converting liquid water into water vapour1 that rises slowly into the atmosphere.

As water vapour rises it cools and condenses around tiny particles, gradually forming clouds2. Precipitation returns water to the surface as rain, snow, sleet or hail3, completing the main stages of the cycle.

Runoff gathers in rivers, lakes and oceans, where the cycle quietly begins again1. In this way the planet maintains a natural balance of water between land, atmosphere and ocean3 — a process every ecosystem depends on.
